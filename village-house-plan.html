Doors and windows all top out at 7'-0", so one lintel band runs around the house. Thai aluminium sliding windows with an MS grill are the usual choice. Put a 2'-0" sunshade (chhajja) over every window. The west (front) windows already sit under the verandah roof.

Notes for the mistri · মিস্ত্রির জন্য

  • Sizes

    Every size on the drawing is the clear room size inside the walls. Walls are extra: 10" outside, 5" inside. That makes the outer size 37'-11" × 35'-6".

  • Heights

    Floor (plinth) at least 2'-6" above ground and above the highest flood seen here. Verandah 2" lower than the rooms. Ceiling 10'-0". Door and window tops at 7'-0".

  • Sun & wind

    The front (west) takes the hot afternoon sun. The 4' verandah shades the front rooms; plant shade trees in the front yard and hang bamboo blinds (chik) on the verandah if it gets too hot. The south breeze enters through Bedrooms 2 and 4. The kitchen on the east gets morning light.

  • Wet areas

    Bath floors 1" lower than rooms and sloped to the drain. All three toilets run north–south (left–right on this drawing). Common bath, basin and kitchen sink sit side by side, so one water line and one drain line serve all three.

  • Kitchen

    Counter 2'-8" high, 2'-0" deep. Stove on the east counter under the window, with the exhaust fan beside it. The summer wind comes from the south-east, so it can push kitchen smoke back into the house: cook on gas indoors and keep the fan running.

  • Skylight

    4' × 4' hole in the roof slab with a 1'-6" raised curb, fixed glass or polycarbonate on top, side louvres to let hot air out, and an MS grill underneath for security.

  • Septic tank

    In the back (east) yard, at least 30 ft (10 m) from the tubewell. More is better, and keep it on the lower side of the ground.

Confirm before building · কাজের আগে ঠিক করুন

  • Common bath

    At 4' × 7' it fits a toilet and a small shower corner, with a 2'-6" door. Bedrooms 1 and 2 at the back share it; Bedrooms 3 and 4 have their own attached baths.

  • Second floor

    If you might build up later, tell the engineer before the foundation. It will need RCC columns and beams now, and space for a stair outside on one side wall.

  • Cooking fuel

    If you cook with wood or straw on a clay stove (chula), put a small open kitchen shed on the north side of the yard. The summer wind then blows its smoke away from the house.

  • Land

    The house plus verandah and steps needs about 38' × 43', plus open space around it for light, air and drainage.

This is a layout plan for agreeing room sizes, doors, windows and furniture fit. It is not a structural drawing. Have a civil engineer size the foundation, roof slab, beams and lintels before construction starts.
