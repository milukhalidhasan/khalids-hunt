# House plan review notes

Review of the hand-drawn plan (`house-sketch-original.jpeg`), 21 Sep 2026.
The house is one storey, in a village, and the front faces **west**.

## What the original sketch showed
- 35 ft wide (12 + 7 + 4 + 12) and 37 ft deep (4 + 14 + 5 + 14), including a 4 ft verandah.
- Four bedrooms, each about 12 × 14 ft, one in each corner.
- A kitchen 7 ft wide, with a 4 ft wide bathroom next to it.
- Two more bathrooms in the 5 ft strip in the middle.
- Dining and drawing room open to each other in the centre, and two separate verandahs at the front.

## What is already good
- Every bedroom is in a corner, so each gets two outside walls with windows and good airflow.
- No bedroom has to be walked through to reach another room.
- Guests come in at the front and the kitchen is at the back, so they stay away from the family area.
- The simple rectangle is cheaper to build.

## The changes (all included in the new plan)
1. **Bathroom doors no longer open into the dining area.** The two middle bathrooms are now attached baths, entered from Bedrooms 3 and 4 (the front bedrooms). The one next to the kitchen stays as the common bathroom.
2. **Kitchen and common bath keep your sketch sizes:** kitchen 7 ft × 10 ft, bath 4 ft × 7 ft. Bedrooms 1 and 2 share the bath, and a wash basin sits just outside it.
3. **Skylight over the dining area.** The dining has no outside wall, so without one it would be dark and hot.
4. **Partition between the drawing room and the dining area.** Its opening is off to one side, so guests at the front door can't see into the dining area or the kitchen.
5. **One joined verandah** across the whole front, 4 ft deep, with steps in the middle.
6. **Outside doors from Bedrooms 1 and 3** in the north (left) wall. Each door sits in the corner and opens flat against the side wall. Bedroom 3's almirah moves to the front wall to make room.

## Because the front faces west
- **West side (the front):** it gets the hot afternoon sun. The 4 ft verandah shades the front rooms, and all the west windows sit under its roof. Plant shade trees in the front yard, and hang bamboo blinds (chik) on the verandah if it gets too hot.
- **East side (the back):** the kitchen and common bath are here, and they get the gentle morning sun.
- **South side:** Bedrooms 2 and 4 and one attached bath catch the south breeze. Bedroom 2, in the south-east corner, is the coolest room.
- **Kitchen smoke:** the summer wind comes from the south-east, so smoke from the east-side kitchen can blow back into the house. Cook on gas indoors and keep the exhaust fan running. For wood or straw cooking, put an outdoor shed on the north side of the yard.
- **Toilets:** all three are set north–south, which is left–right on the drawing.
- **Septic tank:** it goes in the back (east) yard.

## Decide before building
- **Second floor:** will you add one later? If yes, the engineer must plan RCC columns and the foundation now.
- **Cooking fuel:** if you cook with wood or straw, add an outdoor kitchen shed on the north side of the yard.
- **Land size:** you need about 38 ft × 43 ft for the house, verandah and steps, plus open space around it.
